RepairOps offers four base tiers — <TierBadge tier="starter" />, <TierBadge tier="pro" />,
<TierBadge tier="business" />, and <TierBadge tier="enterprise" /> — plus separate AI add-ons. This
page summarizes current pricing, hard limits, feature gates, and AI packaging.

## Plan Overview

| | Starter | Pro | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---------|-----|----------|------------|
| **Monthly Price** | $99 | $199 | $449 | From $1,500 |
| **Annual Price** | $948/yr ($79/mo) | $1,908/yr ($159/mo) | $4,308/yr ($359/mo) | Custom |
| **Best For** | Small single-location shops | One serious shop | Busy teams & light multi-location | Franchises, scale, private cloud |
| **Trial** | 14 days | 14 days | 14 days or sales-assisted | Sales-assisted |

Starter, Pro, and Business are self-serve. Enterprise is sales-assisted (self-serve checkout
refuses it). Every self-serve trial is 14 days and includes 50 trial AI credits to evaluate
AI-powered workflows.

add-on or a credit pack (see below).

## AI Add-Ons

AI is sold separately from the base plan.

| Add-On | Monthly Cost | Mode | Managed Credits / Month | Hard Cap | Best For |
|--------|-------------|------|-------------------------|----------|----------|
| Disabled | $0 | Off | 0 | 0 | No AI features |
| AI Ready | $49 | BYOK | 0 | 0 | Teams bringing their own provider keys |
| AI Assist | $79 | Managed | 200 | 400 | Intake parse, note cleanup, customer summary, KB search |
| AI Copilot | $149 | Managed | 500 | 1,000 | Full managed task coverage (requires Pro+) |
| Enterprise AI | Custom | BYOK + Managed | 2,000 | 5,000 | Self-hosted (Ollama), fallback chains, and audit (Enterprise) |

The **hard cap** is the maximum number of managed credits that can be consumed in a billing period,
even with top-up packs. AI Copilot requires a Pro or higher base plan; Enterprise AI requires
Enterprise and is sales-assisted (its price is set by contract).

## Credit Packs

Need more managed credits than your monthly allocation? Buy a top-up pack at any time.

| Pack | Credits | Price | Cost Per Credit |
|------|---------|-------|-----------------|
| Small | 100 | $15 | $0.15 |
| Medium | 500 | $60 | $0.12 |
| Large | 1,000 | $100 | $0.10 |

Purchased credits are non-refundable and expire after 12 months of inactivity. Monthly included
credits from AI Assist or AI Copilot do **not** roll over.

## Choosing the Right Plan

**<TierBadge tier="starter" /> Starter** suits a single-location shop with a small team. It includes
the full repair workflow, Knowledge Base, iFixit guides, and manual System Builder.

**<TierBadge tier="pro" /> Pro** is the complete operating system for one serious shop: advanced
analytics, the Plugin Marketplace, POS, booking, reviews, and the full AI Gateway control surface.

**<TierBadge tier="business" /> Business** adds team scale and integration: light multi-location,
the REST API and webhooks, Voice Copilot, advanced customer export, and manager insights.

**<TierBadge tier="enterprise" /> Enterprise** is for organizations needing SSO, white-label
branding, self-hosted AI, audit-ready controls, private cloud, or custom limits.
